The AI desk

The model makes the call. The server does the arithmetic.

A language model is a good reader of a market and a poor calculator. So Relay splits the job: the relay computes every level, stop and R, and Claude decides what to do with them.

01 · SCAN

Every fifteen minutes, the whole board

Each time the board refreshes, the relay hands Claude one compact package: the hour, BTC's regime, every perp that moved, and the coins that broke out this morning and have since left the board.

02 · DECIDE

By a playbook, with a grade

The key level, the stops, the targets and the leverage the stop can carry come from code, not prose. The trader grades each setup A, B or C, and a C is no trade.

03 · MANAGE

Read at every quarter-hour close

A health sheet reads the open trade off the board. A close is accepted only when that reading says the trade is broken, not because the model was asked once too often.

A stop only tightens.Nothing the trader can say loosens one. After +1R the relay moves it under the last swing low itself.
Paper first.Every decision is made on the paper account. Real money copies the grades you pick, at the risk you type.

MCP

Plug Claude into your account.

Relay speaks the Model Context Protocol. Make a token under Keys & account → Agent tokens, choose which accounts it may see, and run your own playbook pass from a chat. A full pass is about eight calls.
